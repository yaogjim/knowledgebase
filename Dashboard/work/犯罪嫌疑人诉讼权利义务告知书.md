> **(审查起诉阶段)**
>
> 目前案件已经进入审查起诉阶段，根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》规定， 人民检察院应当在一个月内作出决定，重大、复杂的案件，可以延长十五日；符 合速裁程序适用条件的，应当在十日内作出决定，对可能判处有期徒刑超过一年 的，可以延长至十五日。
>
> 对于监察机关移送起诉的已采取留置措施的案件，人民检察院应当对犯罪嫌 疑人先行拘留，留置措施自动解除。人民检察院应当在拘留后的十日以内作出是 否逮捕、取保候审或者监视居住的决定。在特殊情况下，决定的时间可以延长一 日至四日。人民检察院决定采取强制措施的期间不计入审查起诉期限。
>
> 你在审查起诉阶段依法享有的诉讼权利和承担的诉讼义务如下：
>
> **一** **、诉讼权利**
>
> 1.认罪认罚获得从宽处理的权利
>
> 在接受讯问时，你自愿如实供述自己的罪行，承认指控的犯罪事实，愿意接 受处罚的，可以依法从宽处理。
>
> 2\. 约见值班律师的权利
>
> 犯罪嫌疑人有权约见值班律师，值班律师为犯罪嫌疑人提供法律咨询、程序 选择建议、申请变更强制措施等法律帮助。
>
> 3\. 辩护及获得法律援助的权利
>
> 你在被检察机关第一次讯问后或者采取强制措施之日起，可以聘请律师为你提 供法律咨询，代理申诉、控告。你有权为自己辩护，也可以委托辩护人为你辩护。
>
> 如果你在押或者被监视居住，也可以由你的监护人、近亲属代为委托辩护人； 在此期间你要求委托辩护人的，检察机关应当及时向你的监护人、近亲属或者指 定的人员转达你的要求。

如果你因经济困难或者其他原因没有委托辩护人的，你及你的近亲属可以向 法律援助机构申请指派律师提供辩护。

> 4\. 使用本民族语言文字进行诉讼及获得翻译的权利

你有权使用本民族语言文字进行诉讼。如果你是聋、哑人或者不通晓当地通 用语言文字，检察机关应当为你聘请通晓聋、哑手势或者当地通用语言文字且与 本案无利害关系的人员为你提供翻译。

> 5\. 申请回避的权利

你及你的法定代理人或者辩护人认为检察人员具有法定回避事由的，有权要 求他们回避。你及你的法定代理人或者辩护人对检察机关驳回申请回避的决定， 有权申请复议一次。

> 6\. 核对笔录、讯问知情、亲笔书写供词的权利

讯问笔录应当交给你核对。如果你没有阅读能力，检察人员应当向你宣读。 如果记载有遗漏或者差错，你有权要求补充或改正。

> 如果检察机关对讯问进行同步录音录像，检察人员应当向你告知。
>
> 如果你请求自行书写供述的，检察人员应当准许。
>
> 7\. 知悉用作证据的鉴定意见及申请补充鉴定或者重新鉴定的权利
>
> 检察机关应当向你告知用作证据的鉴定意见。

对于用作证据的鉴定意见，你有权申请补充鉴定或者重新鉴定，但除原鉴定 违反法定程序外，你应当承担补充鉴定或者重新鉴定的费用。

> 8.同意适用速裁/简易程序的权利

对于可以适用速裁/简易程序审理的案件，检察机关在征得你的同意后，可 以建议人民法院适用速裁/简易程序进行审理。

> 9.申请变更及解除强制措施等权利

你及你的法定代理人、近亲属或者辩护人，有权申请变更强制措施；对于检 察机关采取强制措施法定期限届满的，有权要求解除强制措施。

> 如果你被羁押，有权申请检察机关对羁押必要性进行审查。
>
> 10.证明文件知悉权

如果你被传唤到指定地点或住处接受讯问，你有权要求检察人员出示证明文件。

> 1\. 控告、申诉及获得国家赔偿的权利
>
> 对于检察人员侵犯你诉讼权利和人身侮辱的行为，或者办案机关及其工作人 员的违法行为，你有权提出控告或者申诉。
>
> 对办案人员采用刑讯逼供等非法方法收集证据的行为，你有权提出控告。如 果你能够提供涉嫌非法取证的人员、时间、地点、方式和内容等材料或者线索的， 检察机关应当受理并审查。
>
> 如果你的人身权利、财产权利因检察机关及其工作人员违法行使职权而受到 侵犯，你有权要求国家赔偿。
>
> **二** **、诉讼义务**
>
> 1.接受相关诉讼行为的义务
>
> 你应当遵守刑事诉讼法及有关规定，接受检察机关依法采取的强制措施及其 他诉讼行为。
>
> 2\. 不得干扰作证的义务
>
> 你在诉讼中不得隐匿、伪造、毁灭证据或者串供，不得威胁引诱证人作伪证以 及进行其他干扰司法机关诉讼活动的行为；违反前述规定的，将被追究法律责任。
>
> 3\. 接受讯问并在笔录上签名、按要求书写亲笔供词的义务
>
> 你对检察人员的讯问，应当如实回答。如果你认为讯问笔录没有错误，应当 逐页签名、盖章或者捺指印。
>
> 必要的时候，经检察人员要求，你应当亲笔书写供述。
>
> 4\. 接受检查、搜查的义务
>
> 你应当接受为确定你的某些特征或者生理状态而进行的人身检查、提取指纹 信息，采集血迹、尿液等生物样本。

如果你拒绝，检察人员认为必要的时候，可以强制检查。如果你是女性，检 查你的身体应当由女工作人员或者医师进行。

> 你应当接受检察人员为收集犯罪证据而进行的搜查。

> **Notification of the Suspect's Procedural Rights and Obligations**
>
> At present,the case has entered the stage of examination and prosecution.

According to the provisions of the Criminal Procedure Laws of the People's Republic of China,the People's Procuratorate shall make a decision within one month.For serious and complex cases,the time limit may be extended by 15 days.If the conditions for application of the expedited procedures are met,a decision shall be made within 10 days,and for possible sentence of imprisonment for more than one year,it may be extended to 15 days.

For cases that have been transferred by the supervisory authority for prosecution,the People's Procuratorate shall first detain the criminal suspect, and the lien measures shall be automatically lifted.The People's Procuratorate shall make a decision on whether to arrest,release on bail pending trial,or live at home under surveillance within ten days after detention.In special circumstances,the decision time can be extended by one to four days.The period during which the People's Procuratorate decides to adopt compulsory measures shall not be included in the time limit for examination and prosecution.

During the stage of examination and prosecution,your legal procedural rights and obligations are as follows:

> I.Litigious rights

1.Right to get lenient treatment for pleading guilty and accepting punishment.

> During the interrogation,if you voluntarily and truthfully confessed your crime,admitted the facts of the alleged crime,and are willing to accept punishment,you can be punished in a lenient manner.
>
> 2.The right to see a duty lawyer
>
> The criminal suspect has the right to have an appointment with a duty lawyer, who provides legal advice,advice on procedure selection,application for change of compulsory measures and other legal assistance to the criminal suspect.
>
> 3.The right to defend and obtain legal aid
>
> After you are interrogated for the first time by the procuratorial organ or on the day when compulsory measures are taken,you may engage a lawyer to provide you with legal advice and to appeal or accuse on your behalf.You have the right to defend yourself or to entrust a defender to defend you.
>
> If you are in custody or under residential surveillance,your guardian or a close relative may entrust a defender to you on your behalf.If you request to entrust a defender during this period,the procuratorial organ shall promptly convey your request to your guardian,a close relative or a designated person.
>
> If you have not appointed a defender due to financial difficulties or other reasons,you and your close relatives may apply to a legal aid agency for an attorney to provide defense.
>
> 4.The right to use the native language for litigation and obtain translation services
>
> You have the right to sue in your native language.If you are deaf or dumb or do not know the local common spoken and written language,the procuratorial organ shall hire a person who knows the common language of the locality and has no interest in the case to provide an interpreter for you.
>
> 5.The right to apply for withdrawal

If you and your legal representative or defender believe that the prosecutors have legal reasons for withdrawal,you have the right to request them to withdraw.You and your legal representative or defender shall have the right to apply for a reconsideration once the procuratorial organ rejects the decision of the application for withdrawal.

6.The right to check transcripts,know the interrogation and write confessions

The transcript of the interrogation shall be given to you for checking.If you do not have the ability to read,the procurator shall read to you.If there are omissions or errors in the record,you have the right to request supplement or correction.

If the procuratorial organ makes simultaneous audio and video recording of the interrogation,the procuratorial personnel shall inform you.If you request to write your own confession,the prosecutor shall allow it.

7.The right to know the expert opinion used as evidence and the right to apply for supplementary verification or reappraisal

The procuratorial organ shall inform you of the expert opinion used as evidence.

For the expert opinion used as evidence,you have the right to apply for supplementary verification or re-appraisal,but you shall bear the cost of

supplementary verification or re-appraisal unless the original appraisal violates legal procedures.

> 8.Right to agree to expedited/summary procedures

For cases that apply for expedited/summary procedures,the procuratorial organ,after obtaining your consent,can recommend that the people's courts apply expedited or summary procedures for trial.

> 9.Right to apply for changes and lifting of compulsory measures
>
> You and your legal representative,close relatives,or defenders have the right to apply for changes to compulsory measures;if the statutory period for compulsory measures taken by the procuratorial organ expires,you have the right to request the lifting of compulsory measures.
>
> If you are detained,you have the right to apply to the procuratorate to review the necessity of detention.
>
> 10.Right to know the supporting documents
>
> If you are summoned to a designated place or residence for questioning,you have the right to ask the prosecutor to produce supporting documents.
>
> You have the right to file a complaint against the case-handling personnel who collect evidence by extorting confessions by torture.If you can provide information or clues about the person,time,place,method,and content suspected of illegal collection of evidence,the procuratorial organ shall accept and examine it.
>
> If your personal rights and property rights are infringed upon by the procuratorial organ and its functionaries for illegally exercising their functions and powers,you have the right to claim compensation from the state
>
> II.Litigation obligations
>
> 1.The obligation to accept relevant litigation
>
> You shall abide by the criminal procedure law and relevant provisions,and accept the compulsory measures and other litigation actions taken by the procuratorial organ according to law.
>
> 2.The obligation of not interfering with testifying
>
> In a lawsuit,you shall not conceal,forge or destroy evidence or collude in making a confession,or threaten to induce witnesses to give false testimony or engage in any other act of interfering with the proceedings of a judicial organ;

and those who violate the foregoing provisions will be investigated for legal responsibility.

3.The obligation to accept interrogation,sign the transcript,and write a confession as required

You shall answer truthfully to the interrogation of prosecutors.If you think there is no error in the interrogation transcript,you shall sign,seal,or stamp it page by page.

When necessary,at the request of the prosecutors,you should write your own statement.

> 4.The obligation to accept inspections and searches

You shall undergo a physical examination to determine some of your characteristics or physiological status,take fingerprint information,and take biological samples such as blood,urine,etc.

If you refuse to do so,the prosecutors can make mandatory inspections when they deem it necessary.If you are a female,the examination of your body should be performed by a female staff member or a physician.

> You shall be searched by prosecutors to collect criminal evidence.
