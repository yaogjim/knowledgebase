### **关于领事通报的说明书**

> 你在中国被依法限制或者剥夺人身自由期间，希望我们将你被限制或剥夺人身 自由的情况通知你本国驻华使领馆吗?
>
> As a foreign citizen whose personal freedom is legally restricted or deprived of in China,do you want us to notify your country's embassy or consulate in China about your such status?
>
> 我希望将我被限制或剥夺人身自由的情况通知我国驻华使、领馆。I want my country's embassy or consulate in China to be notified about my status as legally restricted or deprived of personal freedom.
>
> □ 我不希望将我被限制或剥夺人身自由的情况通知我国驻华使、领馆。I don't want my country's embassy or consulate in China to be notified about my. status as legally restricted or deprived of personal freedom.
