### **认罪认罚从宽制度告知书**

> 一 、根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第十五条的规定，犯罪嫌疑人、被告 人自愿如实供述自己的罪行，承认指控的犯罪事实，愿意接受处罚的，可以依法从 宽处理。
>
> 二 、犯罪嫌疑人、被告人没有委托辩护人，法律援助机构没有指派律师为其提 供辩护的，由值班律师为犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提供法律咨询、程序选择建议、申请 变更强制措施、对案件处理提出意见等法律帮助，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人有权约见值 班律师。
>
> 三 、适用认罪认罚从宽制度，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人应当签署《认罪认罚从宽制 度告知书》及《认罪认罚具结书》。《认罪认罚具结书》应由辩护人或值班律师签 字确认。有下列情形之一的，不需要签署认罪认罚具结书：
>
> (一)犯罪嫌疑人是盲、聋、哑人，或者是尚未完全丧失辨认或者控制自己行 为能力的精神病人的；

(二)未成年犯罪嫌疑人的法定代理人、辩护人对未成年人认罪认罚有异议的；

> (三)其他不需要签署认罪认罚具结书的情形。
>
> 四、《认罪认罚具结书》应载明：犯罪嫌疑人基本信息、认罪认罚情况、被指 控的犯罪事实、罪名及适用的法律规定、检察机关对犯罪嫌疑人拟提出的从轻、减 轻或者免除处罚等从宽处罚的建议；认罪认罚后案件审理适用的程序及其他需要听 取意见的情形。
>
> 五 、检察机关根据犯罪嫌疑人、被告人的犯罪事实、犯罪情节、认罪情形，就 主刑、附加刑、是否适用缓刑等拟出量刑建议。犯罪嫌疑人、被告人或其辩护人/ 值班律师可以向检察机关提出从轻、减轻处罚等意见，检察机关根据案件情况，可
>
> 以进行调整。
>
> 六 、对于认罪认罚案件，除《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》第二百零一条规定 的除外情形外，人民法院依法作出判决时， 一般应当采纳人民检察院指控的罪名和 量刑建议。
>
> 七、《认罪认罚具结书》签署后，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人提出异议或变更的，人 民检察院将重新提出量刑建议。

八 、经协商，犯罪嫌疑人、被告人如不同意检察机关的量刑建议，有权不签署 《认罪认罚具结书》,不适用本制度。

> **Notification of the System of Leniency for Pleading Guilty and Accepting Punishment**
>
> 1.According to Article 15 of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China,where a criminal suspect or defendant voluntarily and truthfully confesses to his or her crime,admits to the facts of the crime that he or she is charged with,and is willing to accept punishment,the criminal suspect or defendant may be granted leniency in accordance with the law.
>
> 2.The system of leniency for pleading guilty and accepting punishment is applicable.A criminal suspect and defendant shall sign in writing the Notification of the System of Leniency for Pleading Guilty and Accepting Punishment and the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,which shall be valid only after being signed and confirmed by the defender or duty lawyer.
>
> 3.The Affidavit for Confession of Judgement states:the basic information of the criminal suspect,admission of guilt and punishment,the charges alleged and the applicable terms,and the suggestions of the procuratorial organ on the criminal suspect to be given a lighter,mitigated punishment or exemption from punishment; and the procedure applicable to the trial after admission of guilt and other circumstances in which opinions need to be heard.
>
> 4.The procuratorial organ will draw up sentencing proposal based on the circumstances of the crime and confession of the suspect or defendant.If the criminal suspect or defendant has other circumstances prescribed by law or decided at discretion to be given a lighter or mitigated punishment,the range of sentencing shall be appropriately adjusted.The suspect,the defendant or his/her
>
> counsel/duty lawyer may give an opinion to the procuratorial organ on the specific range of sentencing
>
> 5.After the criminal suspect or defendant signs the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,the people's court will generally confirm the criminal facts according to the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement and the contents specified in the corresponding indictment,and the people's court shall generally adopt the sentencing recommendations made by the People's Procuratorate.
>
> 6.After signing the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,a criminal suspect or defendant may request withdrawal,but an application shall be submitted in writing to the case handling authority,and the People's Procuratorate will make a new recommendation for sentencing.
>
> 7.If the criminal suspect or defendant does not make a written application for withdrawal,but raises objections or changes to the main criminal facts,charges and confession statements specified in the indictment confirmed by the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,the criminal suspect or defendant shall be deemed to withdraw the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement.
>
> Where the criminal suspect or defendant withdraws the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement signed by the criminal suspect or defendant cannot be used as the basis for his /her confession and accepting punishment,but it may still be used as the evidence for his /her confession,and the people's court shall determine the facts of the case in combination with other evidence.
>
> After the criminal suspect,the defendant withdraws the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,if the People's Procuratorate agrees to re-sign the Affidavit for Confession of Judgement,the People's Procuratorate shall make a new recommendation on sentencing based on the newly signed Affidavit for Confession of Judgement;If the criminal suspect or defendant withdraws the Affidavit for
