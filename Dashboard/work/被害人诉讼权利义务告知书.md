> **被害人诉讼权利义务告知书**
>
> **(审查起诉阶段)**

根据《中华人民共和国刑事诉讼法》的规定，你作为本案的被害人，在审查 起诉阶段依法享有的诉讼权利和承担的诉讼义务如下：

> **一** **、诉讼权利**
>
> 1.要求提供作证条件和保密的权利

检察机关应当保证你有客观充分地提供证据的条件，并为你保守秘密。如果 你的作证内容涉及国家秘密、商业秘密、个人隐私，你有权要求保密。

> 2\. 委托诉讼代理人和发表诉讼意见的权利

你及你的法定代理人或者近亲属有权委托一至二名律师、人民团体或你所在 单位推荐的人作为诉讼代理人，你的监护人、亲友也可以作为你的诉讼代理人。

检察机关审查案件应当听取你及你的诉讼代理人的意见。你及你的诉讼代理 人有权向检察机关提出书面意见。

犯罪嫌疑人认罪认罚的，检察机关应当对下列事项听取你及你的诉讼代理人 的意见：(1)涉嫌的犯罪事实、罪名及适用的法律规定；(2)从轻、减轻或者 免除处罚等从宽处罚的建议；(3)认罪认罚后案件审理适用的程序；(4)其他 需要听取意见的事项。

> 3\. 使用本民族语言文字进行诉讼及获得翻译的权利
>
> 你有权使用本民族语言文字进行诉讼。

如果你是聋、哑人或者不通晓当地通用语言文字，检察机关应当为你聘请通 晓聋、哑手势或者当地通用语言文字且与本案无利害关系的人员为你提供翻译。

> 4\. 申请回避的权利

你及你的法定代理人或者诉讼代理人认为检察人员具有法定回避事由的，有 权要求他们回避。

> 你及你的法定代理人或者诉讼代理人对检察机关驳回申请回避的决定，有权 申请复议一次。
>
> 5\. 知悉用作证据的鉴定意见及申请补充鉴定或者重新鉴定的权利
>
> 检察机关应当向你或者你的法定代理人、近亲属或诉讼代理人告知用作证据 的鉴定意见。
>
> 对于用作证据的鉴定意见，你或者你的法定代理人、近亲属、诉讼代理人有 权申请补充鉴定或者重新鉴定，但除原鉴定违反法定程序外，你应当承担补充鉴 定或者重新鉴定的费用。
>
> 6\. 控告权
>
> 如果办案人员有侵犯你诉讼权利和人身侮辱的行为，或者采用羁押、暴力、 威胁、引诱、欺骗等非法方法收集证据的行为，你有权提出控告。
>
> 7\. 获得保护的权利
>
> 如果你因在诉讼中作证，你或者近亲属的人身安全面临危险，你有权请求检 察机关予以保护。
>
> 如果你因在危害国家安全犯罪、恐怖活动犯罪、黑社会性质组织犯罪、毒品 犯罪等案件中作证，你或者近亲属的人身安全面临危险，检察机关应当依法为你 采取保护措施。
>
> 8.知悉证明文件、核对笔录和亲笔书写陈述的权利
>
> 你有权要求对你进行询问的检察人员向你出示证明文件。
>
> 询问笔录应当交你核对。如果你没有阅读能力，检察人员应当向你宣读。如 果记载有遗漏或者差错，你有权要求补充或改正。
>
> 你有权请求自行书写陈述，检察人员应当准许。
>
> 9.提起附带民事诉讼的权利
>
> 如果你由于犯罪嫌疑人的犯罪行为而遭受物质损失，符合法定条件的，有权 向人民法院提起附带民事诉讼。提起附带民事诉讼应交民事诉状正本一份、副本

二份。

如果你向人民法院提起附带民事诉讼，在必要的时候，你有权申请人民法院 采取保全措施。

> 10\. 未成年被害人的特殊权利

你若未满十八周岁，询问时将通知你的法定代理人到场，法定代理人可以代 为行使你的诉讼权利。无法通知，法定代理人不能到场或是共犯的，可以要求通 知你的其他成年亲属，所在学校、单位或者居住地的村民委员会、居民委员会、 未成年人保护组织的代表到场。

> 你若是未满十八周岁的女性，询问时应当有女工作人员在场。
>
> **二** **、诉讼义务**
>
> 1.作证的义务
>
> 凡是知道案件情况的人，都有作证的义务。
>
> 2\. 如实提供证据、陈述的义务

你应当如实地提供证据、陈述，诬告陷害、有意作虚假陈述或者隐匿罪证， 将承担相应的法律责任。

> 3\. 在询问笔录上签字和按要求书写陈述的义务
>
> 经核对无误后，你应当在询问笔录上逐页签名、盖章或者捺指印。
>
> 必要的时候，经检察人员要求，你应当亲笔书写陈述。
>
> 4\. 接受检查的义务

你应当接受为确定你的某些特征或者生理状态而进行的人身检查、提取指纹 信息，采集血迹、尿液等生物样本。

> 如果你是女性，检查你的身体应当由女工作人员或者医师进行。

> **Notification of the Procedural Rights and Obligations to the Victims**

According to the relevant provisions of the Criminal Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China,you as the victim of this case shall be entitled to procedural rights and shall assume procedural obligations during the phase of examination and prosecution as follows:

> I.Procedural Rights

1.The right to request conditions and confidentiality for testifying The procuratorial organ shall guarantee the conditions for you to provide evidence objectively and sufficiently and keep your testimony confidential.You shall have the right to request confidentiality of your testimony concerning state secrets, business secrets and personal privacy.

2.The right t entrust agents ad litem and present procedural opinions You, your legal representatives or close relatives shall have the right to entrust one or two attorneys,civil organizations or personnel recommended by your work unit to be your agents ad litem,your guardians or relatives may also serve as your agents ad litem.

The procuratorial organ shall listen to your opinions and your agents ad litem's.You and your agents ad litem shall have the right to present opinions in writing to the procuratorial organ.

3.The right to use your own native language for proceedings and to obtain translation.

You shall have the right to use your own native language for proceedings.If you are deaf or mute,or have no knowledge of the commonly used local language,the procuratorial organ shall hire someone proficient in sign language

> or the commonly used local language who have no interest in the case to translate for you.
>
> 4.The right to apply for withdrawal
>
> Either you,your legal representatives or agents ad litem shall have the right to demand withdrawal of the prosecutors if required to be withdrawn for circumstances provided for under statutory law.
>
> You,your legal representatives or agents ad litem shall have right to apply once more for re-consideration of a withdrawal request which has been rejected by the procuratorial organ.
>
> 5.The right to obtain awareness of expert reports used as evidence and to apply for supplementary verifications or re-verifications.
>
> The procuratorial organ shall inform you,your legal representatives,close relatives or agents ad litem of the expert reports used as evidence.You,your legal representatives,close relatives or agents ad litem shall have the right to apply for supplementary verifications or re-verifications concerning the expert reports used as evidence.Unless the original verification violates the legal procedure, you shall assume the cost for supplementary verifications or re-verifications.
>
> 6.The right to bring charges
>
> You shall have the right to bring charges against prosecutors whose acts infringe your procedural rights or personal dignity or illegally collect evidence through detention,violence,threat,temptation or deceit etc.
>
> 7.The right to be protected
>
> You shall have the right to request protection by the procuratorial organ during the course of testifying when the personal safety of you or your close relatives is endangered.The procuratorial organ shall provide you with protection in conformity with the law when the personal safety of you or your close relatives

is endangered due to your testifying for the crimes concerning national security, terrorist activities,mia-style syndicated crime and drugs etc.

8.The right to obtain awareness of certain certificates,check statement transcripts and write personal statements.You shall have the right to request the identification of prosecutors interviewing you by showing their certificates.

The interview transcripts shall be given to you for checking.The prosecutors shall read them out to you if you cannot read.If there are any omissions or errors, you shall have the right to request supplements or corrections.

You shall have the right to write personal statements,which shall be permitted by the prosecutors.

> 9.The right to file a collateral civil suit

You shall have the right to file a collateral civil suit in the People's Court for the material losses caused by the criminal suspects if the legal conditions are met.An original civil indictment and two copies shall be submitted when a collateral civil suit is filed.

In the event of filing a collateral civil suit in the People's Court,you shall have the right to request adoption of preservation measures by the People's Court if it is necessary.

> 10.Special rights minor victims are entitled

If you are under the age of 18,your legal representatives shall be informed to be present at interview,who can exercise your procedural rights on behalf of you. If your legal representatives is absent or cannot be notified,or if your legal representatives are accomplices,you shall have the right to request notification to other adult relatives or representatives from your school,your work unit, villagers'committee,residents'committee or minors'protection organizations to be present.If you are a female under the age of 18,female staff of the

> procuratorate shall be present during the interview.
>
> Ⅱ.Procedural Obligations
>
> 1.The obligation to testify
>
> Anyone having knowledge of the case shall assume the obligation to testify once they have received such knowledge.
>
> 2.The obligation to truthfully provide evidence and statements
>
> You shall truthfully provide evidence and statements,otherwise you shall be subject to corresponding legal liabilities for false charges and fraudulent charges, intentional false statements or concealment of the criminal evidence.
>
> 3.The obligation to sign the interview transcripts and write statements as required
>
> You shall sign your name or affix a seal or fingerprints to the interview transcripts page by page once the interview transcripts have been checked to be correct.
>
> If it is necessarily required by the prosecutors,you shall write a personal
>
> statement.
>
> 4.The obligation to accept examinations
>
> You shall accept the examinations of your person or body,fingerprints collection,biological sample collection of the blood or urine,which aim to confirm your characteristics or physiological status.
>
> In case of feminine gender,your body examinations shall be conducted by female staff or doctors.
