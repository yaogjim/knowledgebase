# Nik Shevchenko [DON’T DO MARKETING] When I s

**Nik Shevchenko**

\[DON’T DO MARKETING\] When I started

[@omidotme](/omidotme)

I’ve set marketing as my top-1 priority, which was the biggest mistake I’ve ever made as a founder Marketing was by top priority since May 2024 till November 2025 and we didn’t grow much but then everything changed: In ~November I met

[@cmonkey](/cmonkey)

, founder of

[@FrameworkPuter](/FrameworkPuter)

who gave me a simple advice: focus only on product Back then I thought that advice is retarded cuz how can you grow the company if you don’t do marketing But I still decided to try: Framework is doing incredibly well with 0 marketing - I was curious, how? So I made the product my top1 priority: and stopped all growth activities except instagram which I do for fun (same username) Fast forward, 3 months later, we are making 3-4x more revenue per month ($230k) than in November ($70k) - with no marketing 80% of our revenue comes from word of mouth and revenue grows because people see improvements in quality The biggest lesson I learned: marketing isn’t actually a top driver of growth. The top driver is a great product I told this advice to a few of my friends and was surprised how many people still prioritize distribution over a great product Why everyone was surprised: we all want to grow fast but most think that to grow you need to sell a lot, and to sell, marketing should be a top priority Reality is that to grow very fast, you need to build something incredibly fucking good and it will market and sell itself This is how you unlock real growth
