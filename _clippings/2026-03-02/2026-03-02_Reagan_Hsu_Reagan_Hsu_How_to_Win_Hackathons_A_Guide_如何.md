## A Brief Snapshot 简要概述

Almost all of the following advice is grounded in two things: being curious, and being someone who wants to build things. 以下几乎所有建议都基于两件事： 保持好奇心， 以及成为一个想要创造事物的人 。

If you have both, you will inevitably be good at hackathons. Your ideas will wow others, your passion will shine through in demos, and your projects will be built from the perspective of someone building a real product, not someone just trying to win a competition. 如果你同时具备这两点，你必然会在黑客马拉松中表现出色。你的想法会令他人惊艳，你的热情会在演示中闪耀，你的项目会从打造真正产品的角度出发，而不是仅仅为了赢得比赛。

## How to Get Ideas 如何获得灵感

Good ideas are hard to come by, and it's daunting to compete against others who have been thinking about a project for weeks, even months. 好点子难得，而且要和那些已经思考某个项目数周甚至数月的人竞争，更是令人望而生畏。

However, it's more than possible to come up with great ideas in a short time span. Below are some of my tips. 然而，在短时间内想出好点子并非不可能。以下是我的一些建议。

How to Get Ideas Diagram 如何获取创意图

## 1\. Talk to sponsors. 1. 与赞助商洽谈。

They have good ideas on what projects are boring versus innovative, and occasionally will even tell you what they would like to see made. 他们对哪些项目枯燥乏味、哪些项目具有创新性有独到的见解，有时甚至会告诉你他们希望看到什么样的项目被制作出来。

Also, sponsor products are often super cool - allowing you to build things you had never imagined before - yet people foolishly skip over them. 此外，赞助商的产品通常非常酷——让你能够打造以前从未想象过的东西——但人们却愚蠢地忽略了它们。

One personal example is when I tried Convex at Y Combinator - I haven't used any other database in a hackathon since. 我个人的一个例子是，我在 Y Combinator 尝试使用 Convex 数据库之后，就再也没有在黑客马拉松中使用过其他数据库了。

## 2\. Start with a problem. 2. 从问题入手。

The most important aspect of a project is that it actually serves a purpose. Finding a problem to solve is a necessary first step. 项目最重要的方面在于它必须真正服务于某个目的。找到需要解决的问题是必要的第一步。

## 3\. Use personal ideas. 3. 运用个人想法。

Good ideas are often personal ones, and vice versa. You automatically have a valid pain point, an idea of how to solve it, and a customer: yourself. Having these three solves the issue of how most projects keep judges asking: "Who would use this?" and "Why was this made?" 好的想法往往源于个人经历，反之亦然。你自然而然地会发现一个切实存在的痛点，想到一个解决方案，并且拥有一个客户：你自己。有了这三点，就能解决大多数项目让评委不断质疑“谁会用这个？”和“为什么要做这个？”的问题。

## 4\. Don't force originality. 4. 不要强求原创。

For one, do not look at past hackathon projects for inspiration. It becomes hard to think originally after doing so. Also, in reference to the former point, none of them will be truly personal. 首先，不要从以往的黑客马拉松项目中寻找灵感。这样做会让你很难进行原创思考。其次，正如前文所述，这些项目都不可能真正体现个人特色。

Also, do not ask yourself, "How can I be different?" This usually results in products that have no good purpose. Instead, ask yourself, What do I wish existed that currently doesn't? If you cannot find an answer to this question, think harder, because there are definitely many. 此外，不要问自己“我怎样才能与众不同？” 这样做通常会导致产品毫无用处。相反，你应该问自己： 我希望存在但目前不存在的东西是什么？ 如果你找不到答案，那就再仔细想想，因为肯定有很多。

## 5\. Identify inspiration channels. 5. 寻找灵感来源。

Here are mine, which are honestly pretty simple: 以下是我的想法，其实很简单：

\- Writing random shower ideas down in my notes app - 在我的备忘录应用程序中记下一些关于淋浴的随机想法

\- Saving cool-looking UI on Twitter/Instagram - 在 Twitter/Instagram 上保存酷炫的 UI

\- While programming, always asking, "What would save me some time, no matter how small?" - 在编程时，总是问自己：“无论多么微小，怎样才能节省一些时间？”

\- Saving interesting technical concepts that I can apply to projects (e.g. most recently, I read up about WebMCP and thought it was super cool) - 保存一些有趣的科技概念，以便应用到项目中（例如，最近我了解了 WebMCP，觉得它非常酷）。

\- Keeping up with the latest startups in Y Combinator - 随时关注 Y Combinator 的最新创业公司

## How to Plan 如何规划

Planning a good hackathon project goes hand in hand with ideation, but these tips are for after you have an initial concept/direction. 规划一个好的黑客马拉松项目与构思密不可分，但这些技巧适用于你已经有了初步概念/方向之后的情况。

## 1\. Plan a specific user flow, keeping the demo in mind. 1. 规划具体的用户流程，并牢记演示内容。

Have a specific set of steps you will demo, and keep it as short as possible so judges can fully understand your product. 准备一套具体的演示步骤，并尽可能简短，以便评委能够充分理解你的产品。

## 2\. Plan for a visual wow factor. 2. 计划打造令人惊艳的视觉效果。

Unfortunately, it's difficult for judges to understand technical depth in the span of a few minutes, especially if you're using tools they've never heard of. What's universal, though, is appreciation for great-looking UI/UX. 遗憾的是，评委很难在短短几分钟内理解技术深度，尤其是在使用他们从未听说过的工具时。不过，所有评委都普遍欣赏美观的用户界面/用户体验。

## 3\. Aim for first place. 3. 目标是第一名。

A lot of people optimize for sponsor tracks, but in my opinion, you should always go for first place, especially if you're with a good team. Believe in yourself! 很多人都追求赞助商提供的赛道，但依我之见，你应该始终以第一名为目标，尤其是在你拥有一支优秀团队的情况下。相信自己！

## How to Implement 如何实施

- Separate frontend and backend, and link them later. You can mock API endpoints on the frontend. 将前端和后端分开，稍后再将它们连接起来。 你可以在前端模拟 API 端点。
- Keep an "if it works, it works" mentality. Hackathon projects are proofs of concept. Make them work end-to-end, but no need to make implementations pretty. 保持“能用就行”的心态。 黑客马拉松项目是概念验证。确保它们能端到端地运行，但无需追求美观。
- Use coding agents liberally. I typically have ~5 running in parallel. You should probably have even more than this, given you're starting a project from scratch. Dedicate a few to planning, a few to research, a few to ideation, and a few to actual implementation - at all times. 尽可能多地使用编码代理。 我通常会同时运行大约 5 个。考虑到你是从零开始创建一个项目，你可能需要运行更多。始终安排一些用于规划，一些用于研究，一些用于构思，一些用于实际实现。
- If you're going to mock part of your project, mock things that aren't core to your product (e.g. data feeds). For judges, it's simple to tell what has been mocked, and it really hurts your project. 如果你要对项目的某个部分进行模拟，请模拟那些并非产品核心的部分 （例如数据源）。评委很容易就能看出哪些部分被模拟过，这会对你的项目造成很大的负面影响。

\- Only implement additional features that add to the "wow" factor without adding much friction to the user flow. - 只实现那些能增加“哇”的惊艳效果 ，而不会给用户流程带来太多阻碍的附加功能。

\- Reserve at minimum 10 minutes, ideally 20 minutes, to plan your demo. This means you need to submit early, and you'd be surprised at how long it takes to make a high-level, two-minute script. - 请至少预留 10 分钟，最好是 20 分钟来准备您的演示 。这意味着您需要尽早提交，而且您可能会惊讶于撰写一份两分钟的概要脚本需要花费多少时间。

\- Keep your main branch functional. Implement features that may break production on feature branches. 保持主分支功能正常。 将可能导致生产环境崩溃的功能在特性分支上实现。

## How to Demo Your Project 如何演示您的项目

Demos are the final hurdle. Generally, explain with as few words as possible, and let your demo do the talking. 演示是最后一道关卡。一般来说，尽量用最少的语言解释，让演示来说话。

Passion, speaking concisely with confidence, and having good body language are all necessary. 热情、自信简洁的表达以及良好的肢体语言都是必不可少的。

\- Have one short, sweet, and clear sentence for your pitch. Should be your first sentence. - 你的推介词应该简洁明了，用一句话概括，并且作为开场白。

\- Assume the judges know basic principles of full-stack development, and nothing else. Do not go into deep technical detail during your demo unless you are confident you can keep it in under two sentences. You risk confusing the judges. 假设评委只了解全栈开发的基本原理，除此之外一无所知。 除非你确信能用两句话概括清楚，否则不要在演示过程中赘述技术细节，否则可能会让评委感到困惑。

## Extra Tips 额外提示

\- If a hackathon goes overnight, pull the all-nighter! The best ideas often come at 2 am. Also, it's a canonical experience. 如果黑客马拉松要通宵进行，那就熬夜吧 ！ 最好的想法往往在凌晨两点出现。而且，这也是一次经典的体验。

\- A team of 2-3 is optimal. In my experience, four is too many, and going solo makes it hard to be creative and manage workload. I like to separate my team into just frontend and backend, and if you have a third person, just have them help out where needed. 2-3 人的团队最为理想 。以我的经验来看，4 个人太多了，而单打独斗则难以发挥创造力并有效管理工作量。我喜欢把团队分成前端和后端两个部分，如果有第三个人，就让他/她在需要的时候提供帮助。

\- Be willing to reject people from joining your team. Especially non-technical people. In my experience, non-technical people are brought in order to help with presentation skills and ideation - neither of which they contribute to because they don't know how to explain technical details in a simple way, and they have no idea what projects are actually technically possible. 要有拒绝他人加入团队的勇气， 尤其是那些非技术人员。以我的经验来看，引入非技术人员通常是为了提升他们的演讲技巧和创意能力，但他们在这两方面都毫无贡献，因为他们既不擅长用简单易懂的方式解释技术细节，也不清楚哪些项目在技术上是可行的。

\- Don't let a time constraint stop you from trying something. You'd be surprised at how much you can get done in just a few hours. (though I will note that anything less than three gets iffy). 不要让时间限制阻止你尝试新事物。 你会惊讶于几个小时内你能完成多少事情。（不过我要指出的是，少于三个小时就有点不太靠谱了。）

\- Finding good teammates is hard. Here are specific traits I always check off: willing to pull an all-nighter, well-spoken, and technical. 找到好的队友很难。 以下是我总会考虑的几个特质：愿意熬夜、口齿伶俐、技术娴熟。

## Sanity Checks for Your Idea 对你的想法进行合理性检验

Please make sure you can answer all of these questions: 请确保您能够回答以下所有问题：

\- Is it showstopping? Will any engineer see it and go, "That's cool"? - 它会令人惊艳吗？会有工程师看到后说“这太酷了”吗？

\- What problem does it solve? 它解决了什么问题？

\- Who is it for? - 它是为谁准备的？

## My Note to Builders 致建筑商的一封信

Hackathons can change your life. In fact, winning the Y Combinator hackathon is what got me introduced to Browser Use, and now I work here full-time! 黑客马拉松可以改变你的人生。事实上，赢得 Y Combinator 黑客马拉松让我接触到了 Browser Use，现在我在这里全职工作！

Losing hackathons sucks, but it's typically clear what you need to work on when you do. This provides a clear path for improvement, and you will inevitably get better and start winning. 黑客马拉松输了固然令人沮丧，但通常情况下，输了之后你会清楚地发现自己需要改进的地方。这为你指明了一条清晰的提升路径，你最终会进步并开始赢得比赛。

Most importantly, each hackathon is like a day at a startup. You are: 最重要的是， 每次黑客马拉松都像是在创业公司工作一天 。你将：

\- Pushing coding agents to their limits - 将编码代理推向极限

\- Either revamping an existing product or making a completely novel one 无论是对现有产品进行改造，还是开发全新的产品。

\- Learning to be a good teammate under high stress - 在高压环境下学习如何成为一名优秀的团队成员

\- Working long hours, with the understanding that there is a high probability you will lose in the end 长时间工作，并且明白最终很可能失败。

In other words, if you're good at hackathons, you will be good at a startup. 换句话说， 如果你擅长黑客马拉松，你就会擅长创业。

So what are you waiting for? Hack away :) 还在等什么？赶紧开工吧 :)
