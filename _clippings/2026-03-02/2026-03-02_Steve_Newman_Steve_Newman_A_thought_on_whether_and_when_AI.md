# Steve Newman A thought on whether and when AI

**Steve Newman**

A thought on whether and when AI will dramatically transform the world (e.g. 100% GDP growth): Some of the most successful businesses in recent decades got there by doing a full-stack rethink of some major sector, such as retail (Amazon) or space launch + telecoms (SpaceX). They integrated vertically, and did a clean-sheet redesign of everything within their (large) scope. The American Housing Corporation is now trying this for home construction. Full-on explosive impact from AI will occur if/when someone is in a position to do a vertically integrated, clean-sheet redesign of the entire industrial economy, from mining to chip fabrication. If they can do that, they'll find a jurisdiction that will let them pursue it in a "special economic zone" and there will be no friction to get in the way. Short of that, you're interacting with the legacy economy in some fashion. Corollary: if you want to know whether we're approaching explosive growth, monitor how many components of the industrial economy are not yet amenable to being fully automated / refactored in this fashion.
