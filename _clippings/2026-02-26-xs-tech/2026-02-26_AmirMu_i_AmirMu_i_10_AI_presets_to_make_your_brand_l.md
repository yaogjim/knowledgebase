# 

THE MAGIC OF DETAILS

The settings I calculated for you aren’t random numbers - they’re your key for premium typography.

Here’s how it works:

- Tracking / Kerning - the space between letters in your text. Negative values (-3% to -7%) in headlines create tight, luxurious spacing.
 
- Leading - the space between lines of your text. 0.8–0.9 for headlines adds blocky and solid sense, making the headline a strong design element.
 
- Google Fonts choice - All free, web-safe, high-quality fonts I’ve battle-tested in real campaigns for years.
 
- Colors & alignment - not mandatory choices, but catchy, high contrast and hooky for many cases.
