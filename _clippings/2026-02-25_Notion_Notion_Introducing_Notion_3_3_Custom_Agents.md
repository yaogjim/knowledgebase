# Introducing Notion 3.3: Custom Agents

Big news today: Custom Agents are finally here! And they’re pretty different from other AI tools you’ve tried.

First, they’re completely autonomous (no manual prompting). Just give them a job, set a trigger or schedule, and they’ll get it done, 24/7. Your agents can automate task triaging, internal Q&A, daily standups and status reports, and even get you to inbox zero.

Second, they’re designed for teams. Create and share them together. Control all their permissions, just like a real teammate. And integrate them with the tools your teams already uses (Slack, Figma, and more!).

Finally, Custom Agents are super easy to create. Spin one up and let it work while you sleep! Here are a few examples on what they can do:

## 

Q&A agents answer repeat questions

GIF

Instead of answering the same questions in Slack manually, over and over again, you can now give your Custom Agent access to the right sources (Notion, Slack, Email, Calendar, MCP integrations) so agents can answer those questions autonomously. We’ve seen tons of examples with great names like Onboarding Eddie, IT Hero, and Sales Detective.

Ben Levick leads Ops and Internal AI at Ramp and has built lots of Custom Agents. “Our agents answer dozens of nuanced product and enablement questions every day with a high success rate. Teams that used to monitor those channels now just audit responses and feed improvements back into the Custom Agent.”

[Watch Ben’s full story →](https://www.notion.com/customers/ramp)

## 

Task routing agents automatically manage incoming requests

GIF

Instead of triaging across multiple systems, Custom Agents can capture incoming requests, turn them into tasks, and route them to the right owner, so nothing slips and the team stays focused.

Remote’s IT Ops Manager, James Lawley, saved the team 20 hours per week. “We replaced an overgrown system with Custom Agents that triage with >95% accuracy and resolve 25%+ of tickets autonomously, all while keeping Slack and Notion in sync.”

[Watch Simon’s demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5cFKe2H3Qw)

of our “all product feedback” agent (it handles bugs + tickets end-to-end). Or meet

[Scruff](https://www.notion.com/blog/meet-scruff-securitys-new-ai-teammate)

—the agent that saved our security team 6+ hours a week.

## 

Status update agents write reports on your schedule

GIF

No more manually writing updates that take hours. An agent can automate your daily standups, weekly sprint recap, or monthly OKR report. Give it the right context across your tools, and it will make sense of it all.

Morgane Palomares, VP of Marketing at Braintrust, says Custom Agents get her to faster decisions. “One competitive agent posts daily updates, and our customer reference agent sends a weekly summary of top new logos to me and the CEO. Each update saves me 20 minutes a day.”
